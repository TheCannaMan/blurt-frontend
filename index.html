This is the address of your Blurt backend — the small service that talks to Kroger on the app's behalf. Your location (used for finding nearby stores) is set from the main screen, not here.

Say or type something and Blurt pulls real matches from your nearest Kroger-family store (Fred Meyer, QFC, Ralphs, etc.) — live from Kroger's product API. You can blurt more than one item at once, like "carrots and potatoes." Pick a price and it collapses down to your choice; Blurt will periodically recheck and flag it if a better price shows up. Nothing here is a real cart — it's a running estimate. Aisle numbers, when Kroger has them mapped for a store, let you sort your list into a rough shopping order. Walmart and Target aren't connected — neither has a free public pricing API.
