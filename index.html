This is the address of your Blurt backend — the small service that talks to Kroger on the app's behalf. Your location (used for finding nearby stores) is set from the main screen, not here.

Sync across devices

Optional — sign in with Google to keep your list and recipes synced across devices. Never required; everything works fully signed out, stored on this device only. Your shopping location is never synced, even signed in.

Backup

Everything lives on this device only. Export a backup file to keep somewhere safe or move to another device — import it there to restore.

Say or type something and Blurt pulls real matches from your nearest Kroger-family store (Fred Meyer, QFC, Ralphs, and other Kroger banners) — live from Kroger's product API. You can blurt more than one item at once, like "carrots and potatoes." Pick a price and it collapses down to your choice; Blurt will periodically recheck and flag it if a better price shows up. Nothing here is a real cart — it's a running estimate. Aisle numbers, when Kroger has them mapped for a store, let you sort your list into a rough shopping order.

About your location: Blurt doesn't remember it between visits, so if it's been about an hour the location bar will gently highlight and suggest tapping Update — handy if you built your list at home and are now shopping somewhere else. Tapping Update re-checks the nearest store, keeps any picks that are still available there, and reopens anything that isn't as a fresh choice.

Voice/typed commands: "clear list", "refresh location", "recheck prices", "clear picked up", "pick cheapest for all", "what's my total", "set budget to $50", "increase/decrease budget by $10", "clear budget", "what's my budget", "recipe for chicken parmesan" (jumps to Recipe Search), "surprise me" (rolls a random recipe), and "go to shopping/recipes/saved" all work as spoken or typed commands instead of being added as items.
